Mid-Atlantic refrigeration project watch

Four regional records for industrial refrigeration sales and estimating, ranked by the next action they justify. Each card links to its evidence.

Gateway Flex Center · Pedricktown, NJ

On Nov. 2, Real Estate NJ reported that J.G. Petrucci had started vertical construction on the 107,000-square-foot industrial shell at 5 Industrial Way. The developer said it would leave the slab unpoured to accommodate underground plumbing for uses such as cold storage and food processing.

Next step. Ask Cushman & Wakefield’s leasing team whether they have a refrigerated tenant in discussion and who would own mechanical design for a fit-out. Hold estimating time until the use and decision-maker are confirmed.

Still open. A refrigerated tenant, refrigeration scope, and bid timing are unconfirmed.

Watch · dated trigger

Garrison Oak cold storage · Dover, DE

The proposed 165,000-square-foot warehouse went back on the Nov. 16 Planning Commission agenda. City staff reported that the existing extension ran through Nov. 30 and the applicant refiled because construction was unlikely to begin by then.

Next step. Check the Nov. 16 disposition and any subsequent permit or filing that identifies the refrigeration design team. Hold estimating time pending that evidence.

Still open. The hearing outcome, refrigeration specifier, and procurement timetable are unverified.

Nov. 16 commission packet · Dover project history

Watch · older signal

Newfield Land cold storage · Newfield, NJ

Franklin Township’s Feb. 17 minutes record conditional preliminary site-plan approval for a proposed 200,200-square-foot cold-storage facility at 1520 Harding Highway. The minutes record the applicant’s intent to return with architectural plans for final review.

Next step. Look for the final site-plan filing with architectural plans. Revisit when the record identifies an end user or design team, or clarifies who owns the refrigeration scope.

Still open. A later final approval, named end user, and refrigeration package were unverified in this review.

Feb. 17 Planning Board minutes, SP25-04

Pass on new construction

ColdTrack fulfillment · Mount Laurel, NJ

ColdTrack announced the opening of its temperature-controlled fulfillment facility on Nov. 17.

Next step. Keep it on the operator map. Revisit on sourced evidence of a service opportunity, retrofit, or expansion.

Still open. The opening announcement establishes no current refrigeration package.
