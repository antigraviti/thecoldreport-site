Franklin Township (Newfield mailing address), New Jersey

Newfield Land cold storage at 1520 Harding Highway

Franklin Township granted conditional preliminary approval for a proposed 200,200-square-foot cold store.

Last checked November 19, 2026 · The Cold Report Editorial Desk

Supplier read

Wait for an operator or final architectural plans before scoping equipment.

Project stage
Preliminary approval granted · Site marketed

Refrigeration
Cold-storage use is in the filing. Building design and refrigeration depend on the eventual operator.

Watch next
Watch for a signed final action, an end user, or architectural plans under SP25-04.

File or reference
Franklin Township SP25-04

What the sources say

  • Franklin Township’s February 17 Planning Board minutes record a 7–0 vote granting conditional preliminary site-plan approval for SP25-04, block 7202, lot 9.
  • The developer told the board there was no end user at the hearing. The board’s minutes describe a return for final site plan with architectural drawings.
  • An October broker listing still described the 200,200-square-foot building as proposed and marketed the site for sale.

What this could mean for suppliers

The municipal record and the listing establish a specific site worth following. A future operator’s temperature ranges and throughput would drive the refrigeration design. Treat marketing descriptions of entitlements as a prompt to check the signed township record.

Still to verify

Signed final disposition, current tenant or purchaser commitment, system design and bid schedule.

Source documents

  1. Franklin Township Planning Board minutes, February 17, 2026, pp. 4–7
  2. October broker listing for the proposed building, via PropertyShark
