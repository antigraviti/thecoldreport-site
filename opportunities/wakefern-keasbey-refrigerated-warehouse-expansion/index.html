Keasbey, New Jersey

Wakefern refrigerated warehouse expansion

Wakefern proposes adding 90,440 square feet of refrigerated warehouse space at 500 Riverside Drive.

Last checked November 19, 2026 · The Cold Report Editorial Desk

Supplier read

Follow the December 2 hearing for the refrigeration design team.

Project stage
Municipal hearing scheduled

Refrigeration
Refrigeration for a perishable distribution addition. Temperature zones and equipment specifications are unconfirmed.

Watch next
December 2, 2026, at 6 p.m. Eastern. Check the Woodbridge hearing for conditions, named design teams and the next filing.

File or reference
Woodbridge P26-20 · NJDEP 1225-26-0002.1-LUP-260002

What the sources say

  • Woodbridge lists Wakefern Food Corp. as applicant for preliminary and final major site-plan approval at 500 Riverside Drive, block 114, lot 2.01. The proposal also changes loading, parking and circulation.
  • The Woodbridge agenda lists the December 2 hearing as a continuation from October 21. It is scheduled in the Council Chambers at 1 Main Street in Woodbridge.
  • NJDEP lists approval on October 27 for the project’s flood-hazard permits. The state entry rounds the expansion to about 91,000 square feet and lists 5000 Riverside Drive. The township filing supplies the 500 Riverside Drive address and exact proposed area used here.

What this could mean for suppliers

The environmental approval gives suppliers a second public record to follow. The municipal hearing is the next opportunity to look for revised plans or testimony naming who controls design. Likely scopes include industrial refrigeration, controls and insulated construction. Confirm the design lead and package timing before allocating estimating hours.

Still to verify

Municipal disposition, temperature set points, refrigerant, procurement owner and any bid date.
