Elizabeth, New Jersey

FreezPak cold storage at Amboy Avenue

Elberon and Saoud broke ground on a roughly 146,000-square-foot build-to-suit for FreezPak at Amboy Avenue and South Front Street.

Last checked November 19, 2026 · The Cold Report Editorial Desk

Supplier read

Check the package award status before pursuing new-build work.

Project stage
Groundbreaking reported

Refrigeration
Purpose-built temperature-controlled storage. Equipment awards and commissioning scope are unconfirmed.

Watch next
Check for named mechanical trades and package awards. Reported delivery target is Q4 2027.

File or reference
Elizabeth Ordinance 6311

What the sources say

  • October trade coverage reports a groundbreaking for Elberon Development Group and Saoud Development’s FreezPak build-to-suit at Amboy Avenue and South Front Street.
  • Elizabeth lists Ordinance 6311 for its financial agreement with SDE Bayway Urban Renewal LLC. FreezPak separately lists another Elizabeth location projected to open in 2027.
  • The reported delivery target is the fourth quarter of 2027.

What this could mean for suppliers

Groundbreaking makes it worth confirming which scopes have already been awarded. A supplier should establish the status of the main refrigeration package before spending estimating time. Controls integration, commissioning or service would require separate confirmation.

Still to verify

Mechanical contractor and refrigeration supplier, procurement status for specific packages and commissioning dates.
