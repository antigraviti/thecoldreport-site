Bethlehem, Pennsylvania

Freshpet cold-storage distribution facility

A 216,000-square-foot warehouse developed by J.G. Petrucci and BGO Cold Chain is fully leased to Freshpet.

Last checked November 19, 2026 · The Cold Report Editorial Desk

Supplier read

Follow commissioning and handover for a possible service lead.

Project stage
Nearing completion

Refrigeration
Dedicated cold storage for refrigerated pet food. Handover is the immediate milestone.

Watch next
Verify year-end completion and operator handover, then look for evidence of recurring service needs.

File or reference
757 Hellertown Road

What the sources say

  • Developer J.G. Petrucci identifies the cold-storage development for Freshpet as 216,000 square feet at 757 Hellertown Road.
  • The Lehigh Valley Economic Development Corporation identifies it as Freshpet’s first dedicated cold-storage facility, for distributing temperature-sensitive pet food.
  • November 13 trade coverage reported that the project was nearing completion, identified Iron Hill Construction Management as general contractor and cited a year-end completion target.

What this could mean for suppliers

The appropriate question at this stage is who will own refrigeration service after handover and when any service contract would be renewed. Allocate estimating time only if a specific service or upgrade scope surfaces.

Still to verify

Actual handover date, commissioning status, installed refrigeration specifications and service contract owner.
