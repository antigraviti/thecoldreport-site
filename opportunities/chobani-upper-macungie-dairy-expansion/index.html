Upper Macungie Township, Pennsylvania

Chobani dairy plant expansion

The township’s November 5 action concerns a 62,260-square-foot addition at Chobani’s planned dairy campus.

Last checked November 19, 2026 · The Cold Report Editorial Desk

Supplier read

Track the milk-receiving addition through final plan review.

Project stage
Preliminary land-development approval reported

Refrigeration
Milk receiving and storage point to potential process cooling and controls work. Package scope is unconfirmed.

Watch next
Watch for the final land-development filing and process utility or mechanical documents at 7356 Industrial Boulevard.

File or reference
Upper Macungie Resolution 2026-48

What the sources say

  • Pennsylvania announced Chobani’s $1.2 billion dairy operation in September, and Chobani identified 7356 Industrial Boulevard as the location of its planned 1.5-million-square-foot campus.
  • Lehigh Valley Press reports that township supervisors granted preliminary approval on November 5 under Resolution 2026-48 for a 62,260-square-foot addition to an existing building at that address. The reported plan includes milk-receiving bays adjacent to 28 storage silos.
  • The 62,260-square-foot figure refers to the addition before the township. The 1.5-million-square-foot figure refers to the announced campus.

What this could mean for suppliers

Milk receiving is the best current clue for process cooling, utility integration and controls. Watch for an engineer of record or design-build team that can confirm which scopes are being bought and how the addition connects to existing equipment.

Still to verify

Final land-development approval, process refrigeration design, package boundaries and procurement timing.

Source documents

  1. Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, September 1 investment announcement
  2. Chobani campus announcement, September 1
  3. Lehigh Valley Press, November 5 township action
  4. Upper Macungie Township agendas and minutes
