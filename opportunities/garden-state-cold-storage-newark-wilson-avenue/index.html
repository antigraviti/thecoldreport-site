Newark, New Jersey

Garden State Cold Storage automated Newark expansion

Trade reporting identifies a 156,000-square-foot automated cold-storage development at 474 Wilson Avenue.

Last checked November 19, 2026 · The Cold Report Editorial Desk

Supplier read

Look for a building permit for the new automated warehouse.

Project stage
Municipal approval reported · Start unverified

Refrigeration
Reported plans include roughly 124,000 square feet of refrigerated and freezer space.

Watch next
Check for a new-building permit at 474 Wilson Avenue or a public construction or design-team announcement.

File or reference
474 Wilson Avenue

What the sources say

  • NJBIZ reported in December 2025 that Newark cleared a new 160-foot-tall automated cold-storage development for Garden State Cold Storage.
  • Jersey Digs reported in January 2026 that about 124,000 square feet would be refrigerated or freezer warehouse space and described about 28,000 square feet of loading areas.
  • Garden State Cold Storage also uses 474 Wilson Avenue in its current site directory. Future permit checks should identify work on the new vertical warehouse.

What this could mean for suppliers

An automated freezer could involve coordination between refrigeration, insulated construction and automated storage. Prioritize documents that name the construction and automation teams or show how equipment suppliers will be selected.

Still to verify

Municipal application and permit identifiers, construction start, system specifications and bid timing.

Source documents

  1. NJBIZ coverage of the reported approval, December 12, 2025
  2. Jersey Digs reporting and renderings, January 9, 2026
  3. Garden State Cold Storage location directory
