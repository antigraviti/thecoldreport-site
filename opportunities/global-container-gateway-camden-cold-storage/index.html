Mid-Atlantic project tracker

Camden, New Jersey

Global Container Gateway multi-temperature facility

West Wing describes a roughly 100,000-square-foot Camden food processing and cold-chain facility as a build-to-suit concept.

Last checked November 19, 2026 · The Cold Report Editorial Desk

Supplier read

A tenant commitment or site-plan filing would move this beyond concept.

Project stage
Public build-to-suit concept

Refrigeration
The concept names centralized refrigeration for frozen, chilled and food-processing areas.

Watch next
Look for a named occupier, an identified parcel, or a municipal site-plan application before pursuing.

File or reference
West Wing project profile

What the sources say

  • West Wing Corporation’s project profile describes a build-to-suit concept in Camden combining food processing, packaging, frozen and chilled storage and distribution.
  • The profile describes centralized refrigeration and several potential temperature zones. The published technical details are concept specifications.

What this could mean for suppliers

The proposed mix of temperature-controlled uses makes the concept relevant to industrial refrigeration design. A named occupier would allow suppliers to assess the actual load, scope and delivery schedule.

Still to verify

Street address or parcel, municipal application, committed operator, funding, construction schedule and procurement status.
