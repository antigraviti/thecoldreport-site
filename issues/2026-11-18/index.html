Dover’s 165,000-square-foot cold-storage plan returns to review

Dover put a proposed 165,000-square-foot cold-storage warehouse back on its planning calendar. The November 16 agenda is a signal to watch for refrigeration suppliers. The commission’s disposition and any procurement timetable remain unverified.

Dover, Delaware · Watch

Dover’s planning staff agenda says the project at 451 Garrison Oak Drive received final site-plan approval in September 2025 and was seeking reapproval under file S-26-16. The city identified SLS Equity Partners as the contract purchaser. The November 16 meeting agenda listed the review.

An estimating team can put this on its watchlist. The next checks are the vote and its conditions, followed by a building permit or another filing that identifies the design team. The project record separates confirmed planning facts from likely spending categories and open procurement questions.

See the Dover Radar record.

What’s already coming online

  • Mount Laurel, New Jersey. ColdTrack announced the opening of its new temperature-controlled fulfillment site on November 17. This is evidence of regional capacity coming online. The announcement establishes no open equipment package.
  • Bethlehem, Pennsylvania. BGO and J.G. Petrucci said in August that Freshpet leased the full 216,000-square-foot development and that completion was scheduled for year-end. The announcement gives market context. Any later buying opportunity needs its own evidence.
